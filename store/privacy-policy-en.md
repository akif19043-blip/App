# Privacy Policy — Dörtyol

_Last updated: 2026-11-01_

Dörtyol itself collects no personal data: there is no server of ours, and
nothing about you is sent to us. The game does contain **rewarded ads**, which
play only when you tap a button and choose to watch one. They are provided by
**Google AdMob**, and Google collects the data described below.

## What the app stores

The game saves your progress — best score, coins, which cars you own, the parts
and paint fitted to them, your settings, and when you last claimed an ad
reward — in local storage on your device. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not readable by us or by anyone else,
- is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game loads and shows ads with the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To load, show
and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

- your device's **advertising ID** and similar device identifiers,
- your **IP address**, and the **approximate location** derived from it,
- your **interactions with ads** (impressions, taps, views),
- device and app information, and **crash and performance data**.

This data goes directly to Google; we do not receive it and cannot see it.
How Google uses it:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

**What you control:**

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for
  your consent to ads when it first starts. You can change your choice at any
  time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings
  (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
- Watching an ad is entirely optional. The whole game can be played without
  ever tapping an ad button.

Loading ads needs an internet connection. The game itself works fully
offline; without a connection the ad buttons simply do not appear.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No in-app purchases.
- No analytics, tracking code or server of our own.
- No access to your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.

## Permissions

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — only to load ads.
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` — to read the advertising ID;
  added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- `VIBRATE` — used only for the in-game vibration feedback you can turn off in
  Settings.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content
rated for general audiences or parental guidance. The game has no purchases
and no communication features.
