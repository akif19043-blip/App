# Privacy Policy — Dörtyol

_Last updated: 2026-09-10_

Dörtyol does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

The game saves your progress — best score, coins, which cars you own, the parts
and paint fitted to them, and your settings — using your device's local browser
storage inside the app. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not readable by us or by anyone else,
- is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- No network requests. Every model, script and sound ships inside the app, so
  it works with no connection at all.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

## Permissions

- `INTERNET` — declared by the app framework. The game makes no network
  requests; nothing is sent or received.
- `VIBRATE` — used only for the in-game vibration feedback you can turn off in
  Settings.

## Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases and no communication features, and
collects nothing. It is suitable for all ages.
