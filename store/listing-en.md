## Full description (max 4000)
Dörtyol is a calm, quick-to-play driving game for phones. No ads, no in-app
purchases, no internet needed.

**Free Roam**
Drive a grid-street city however you like. The traffic lights actually work:
cars queue at red, pull away on green and take turns at junctions. People walk
the pavements, and you can drive into the car parks and poke around.

Collect the coins strung along the streets and run deliveries against the
clock. The time you get is based on the route you will really drive — the
streets are a grid, so it is measured along them, not as the crow flies — and
arriving on time pays a bonus. When the drop-off is off screen an arrow at the
edge points to it, and tapping the minimap zooms out to the whole city.

**Traffic Rush**
Endless highway, one score. Going fast is worth more, and slipping past traffic
with inches to spare is worth more still.

**Garage**
Spend your coins on one of three cars, then fit an engine, gearbox, tyres and
brakes, and pick a colour. Every car remembers its own parts and paint.

**Controls**
On-screen buttons, drag-to-steer, or tilt. There is a left-handed layout, and
a gamepad works if you have one. Plays in portrait and landscape.

**It adapts to your phone**
The game watches its own frame rate and steps the visuals down if your device
is struggling. You can also turn shadows, music and vibration off yourself.

Every 3D model in the game is generated from code with Blender.
